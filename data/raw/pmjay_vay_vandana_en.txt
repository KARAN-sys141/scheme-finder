Ayushman Vay Vandana (PM-JAY for senior citizens) - fact sheet
(Compiled in own words from public sources. Verify on the official PM-JAY portal.)

Details
Since late 2024, Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY has been extended to all senior citizens aged 70 years and above. They get a separate Ayushman Vay Vandana card. The 70+ cover does not depend on income or on the SECC 2011 list.

Benefits
Health cover of Rs 5 lakh per year, cashless at empanelled hospitals.
If the senior citizen already belongs to a family covered under PM-JAY, he or she gets an additional top-up of up to Rs 5 lakh per year for self. This is not shared with other family members.
If the senior citizen is not in an existing PM-JAY family, the Rs 5 lakh cover is given on a family basis.

Eligibility
All citizens aged 70 years and above, irrespective of income or social and economic status.

Application Process
Apply online on the PM-JAY portal or through the Ayushman app, or on the beneficiary portal (beneficiary.nha.gov.in).
Enter the Aadhaar number, verify with Aadhaar OTP, give consent and complete eKYC, then download the Ayushman Vay Vandana card.
The PM-JAY helpline number is 14555.
