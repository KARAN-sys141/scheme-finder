Mukhyamantri Amrutum (MA) Yojana - fact sheet
(Compiled in own words from myScheme.gov.in and other public sources. Verify on the official page.)

Details
Mukhyamantri Amrutum (MA) Yojana is a health scheme of the Government of Gujarat. It was started in 2012 for families below the poverty line (BPL). It gives cashless treatment for serious illnesses that need hospital admission, surgeries and therapies, through a network of empanelled hospitals. Gujarat has also linked MA with the central Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY, so one card works for both in the state.

Benefits
Cover of Rs 5 lakh (₹5,00,000) per family per year.
Treatment is cashless at empanelled hospitals.
There is no limit on the size of the family or the age of members.
The cover renews every year. Unused amount does not carry forward.
Travel expense of up to Rs 300 per hospitalisation is also paid.

Eligibility
Families listed in the BPL list of the Gujarat State Government are eligible under MA Yojana.
Families that are marginally above the poverty line can be covered under MA Vatsalya Yojana if they meet any of these conditions:
Annual family income is up to Rs 4 lakh (₹4,00,000).
A member is an Accredited Social Health Activist (ASHA) worker.
A member is an accredited reporter.
A member is a Class-3 or Class-4 employee on fixed payroll of the State Government.
Senior citizens whose family has a total annual income of up to Rs 6 lakh (₹6,00,000) are covered.
The applicant must be a resident of Gujarat. There is no minimum or maximum age.

Documents Required
Identity proof such as Aadhaar card.
Address proof such as ration card.
Income certificate.
BPL certificate or BPL card (for BPL families).
Passport size photograph and mobile number.

Application Process
Local authorities (taluka offices and civic centres) keep a list of eligible families, mainly those with income below Rs 4 lakh.
Eligible families are enrolled at the designated centres, where the MA card is made. The card needs to be renewed regularly.
Families can also check their status on the official MA Gujarat website or through the Ayushman Bharat card process in the state.
